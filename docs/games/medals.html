Medals

At the end of each competitive game, 🥇 gold, 🥈 silver, and 🥉 bronze medals are awarded to the top 3 performers. Medals accumulate across every game you play during a session.

Per-Game Podium

Each game's result screen shows its own podium with medals displayed next to the winners' names.

Medal Tally

A running medal tally is displayed between rounds and at the end of each game.

Tie Rules

When players tie, medals follow standard tie rules:

  • Two or more players tied for 1st — skip silver; bronze goes to whoever is next.
  • Three or more tied for 1st — skip silver and bronze entirely.
  • Two or more tied for 2nd — skip bronze.
