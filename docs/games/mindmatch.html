Mind Match

Match minds with your fellow players.

Everyone fills in the same blank hoping to match with other players.

Players: 3+ (recommended 4–10). No upper limit.

Tutorial

Gameplay — Submit Phase

Each round presents a fill-in-the-blank prompt. Submit your word secretly.

Scoring

Matches are revealed round by round..

A pair match scores 3 points each.

Larger groups score 1 each.

Unique answers score 0.

Claims

If your word is similar to another, you may be offered a claim. Be careful, you may already be in a pair! Players vote to decide.

You have been offered two claims choose one, or skip.

One or more other players have been offered a claim.

This claim looks like a legitimate typo but it might impact your score if you accept.

You already made this claim, so your vote is automatically to accept.

See if you made the right decision to claim. It was a good decision in this instance.

Without a claim, you would have received 0. If only one of the claims was accepted, you would have received 3.

Leaderboard

The running total is shown throughout the game.

The winner is the first to reach the target score.

Rules

  • Prompt: Each round shows a phrase with a blank to fill in.
  • Submit: Secretly submit your word hoping to match with other players!
  • Match: Match with exactly one other player: 3 points each. Match with a group of 3+: 1 point each. Unique answers score 0.
  • Claims: You may claim your word matches another similar word. Other players vote to decide.
  • Winner: First to reach the target score wins!
