Nine Dash

Nine letters, endless words.

Make as many words as you can from the available 3x3 grid of letters before time runs out.

Players: Recommended 2–8. No upper limit.

Tutorial

Gameplay

Tap letters on the grid to build words, then tap Submit. Selected letters are highlighted in blue. Use Clear to start over or Backspace to remove the last letter.

Start looking for words! Remember the 9-letter word is worth double points!

Successful submissions turn the input box green.

Words using the wrong letters are rejected and shown in red.

A word that has already been taken by another player is also rejected.

Voting

When time's up, vote to accept or reject other players' words. The words are anonymised and shown in order of submission time. A word is rejected if it receives 50% or more downvotes.

Vote on other players' words to accept or reject them.

Your votes have been submitted, waiting for other players.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows how each player performed and gives a breakdown of your submissions.

Rules

  • Grid: A 3x3 grid of nine letters is revealed.
  • Submit: Tap letters on the grid to build words, then tap Submit. Each letter can be used once per word.
  • Scoring: Every accepted word scores one point per letter.
  • Bonus: The hidden nine-letter word scores double (18 points) if you find it!
  • Voting: When time's up, vote to accept or reject other players' words. Words that receive too many downvotes are removed.
  • Winner: Highest score wins!
