Category Clash: Quick Fire

One letter, one category, most words wins.

Race to submit as many words in the chosen category that start with the given letter as you can before time runs out.

Players: Recommended 2–8. No upper limit.

Rules

  • Setup: Admin chooses a category and a random letter is given.
  • Submit: Submit as many words as you can that start with the letter and fit the category.
  • Be quick: You can't submit a word that another player has already submitted.
  • Voting: When time's up, vote to accept or reject other players' words. Words that receive more than 50% downvotes are removed.
  • Winner: The player with the most accepted words wins!

Tutorial

Gameplay / Voting

When it's your turn, submit words that match the category and start with the letter. Only players who submitted at least one word can vote.

The input turns green for accepted words.

If your word is rejected or invalid, the input turns red.

Vote on other players' words to accept or reject them.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard tracks accepted words per player.
