Category Clash: Quick Fire

One letter, one category, most words wins.

Race to submit as many words in the chosen category that start with the given letter as you can before time runs out.

Rules

  • Setup: Admin chooses a category and a random letter is given.
  • Submit: Submit as many words as you can that start with the letter and fit the category.
  • Be quick: You can't submit a word that another player has already submitted.
  • Voting: When time's up, vote to accept or reject other players' words. Words that receive too many downvotes are removed.
  • Winner: The player with the most accepted words wins!
