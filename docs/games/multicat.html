Category Clash: Multicat

Multiple categories, one shared letter.

Submit one word per category, all starting with the given letter.

Rules

  • Setup: Admin chooses multiple categories, and a random letter is given.
  • Submit: Submit one word per category, all starting with the given letter.
  • Update: You may change your answers before the timer ends.
  • Duplicate: You cannot use a word twice or at all if another player has already used it.
  • Voting: When time's up, vote to accept or reject other players' words. Words that receive too many downvotes are removed.
  • Winner: The player with the most accepted words wins!
