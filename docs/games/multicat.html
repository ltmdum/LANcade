Category Clash: Multicat

Multiple categories, one shared letter.

Submit one word per category, all starting with the given letter.

Players: Recommended 2–8. No upper limit.

Tutorial

Gameplay / Voting

Submit one word per category, all starting with the given letter. You can change your answers until the timer expires.

Vote on other players' words to accept or reject them.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows scores and gives a breakdown of who beat you to a word and which players rejected your words.

Rules

  • Setup: Admin chooses multiple categories, and a random letter is given.
  • Submit: Submit one word per category, all starting with the given letter.
  • Update: You may change your answers before the timer ends.
  • Duplicate: You cannot use a word twice or at all if another player has already used it.
  • Voting: When time's up, vote to accept or reject other players' words. Words that receive more than 50% downvotes are removed.
  • Winner: The player with the most accepted words wins! Ties are possible.
