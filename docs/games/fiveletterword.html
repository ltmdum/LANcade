5 Letter Word

Race to guess the word before anyone else!

Everyone races to guess a 5-letter word in 6 tries. First to solve wins!

Players: No upper limit.

Tutorial

Gameplay

Guess the word letter by letter. Green means correct position, yellow means wrong position, and gray means the letter isn't in the word.

3 yellow letters means 3 letters are in the word but in the wrong position.

The mini display shows the leader is almost there with 1 green and 4 yellow!

Winner

The first player to guess the word wins.

Rules

  • Goal: Guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 attempts.
  • Green: Correct letter in the right position.
  • Yellow: Correct letter in the wrong position.
  • Gray: Letter is not in the word.
  • Race: Everyone plays simultaneously. Be the first to solve it!
  • Leaderboard: A mini display shows the current leader's result on each row.
