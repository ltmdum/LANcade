Last Word Standing

Race the clock and survive the votes.

1 player is chosen to submit a word that fits the category and the assigned letter. Last player standing wins!

Rules

  • Turns: Players get assigned turns. When it's your turn, you get a random letter.
  • Submit: Submit a word that fits the category and starts with that letter before time runs out.
  • Vote: Other players vote to accept or reject your word.
  • Penalty: If rejected, you get one more chance with half the time.
  • Elimination: Run out of time or get rejected twice and you're eliminated.
  • Winner: Last player standing wins!
