Last Word Standing

Race the clock and survive the votes.

One player is chosen to submit a word that fits the category and the assigned letter. Last player standing wins!

Players: 2+ (recommended 4–8). No upper limit.

Rules

  • Turns: Players get assigned turns. When it's your turn, you get a random letter.
  • Submit: Submit a word that fits the category and starts with that letter before time runs out.
  • Vote: Other players vote to accept or reject your word. 50% or more downvotes gets your word rejected.
  • Penalty: If rejected, you get one more chance with half the time.
  • Elimination: Run out of time or get rejected twice and you're eliminated.
  • Winner: Last player standing wins!

Tutorial

Gameplay

When it's your turn, you have until the timer runs out to submit a category word starting with your assigned letter.

The input is ready for your submission.

A word has been typed and is ready to submit.

After submitting, wait for other players to vote.

When it's someone else's turn...

Wait and watch their countdown.

Vote to accept or reject their submitted word.

Elimination

If your first submission gets rejected, you are given a new letter with half the time allowance.

If you receive two rejections or run out of time, then you're eliminated.

Winner

The last player standing is crowned the winner.

Notes

  • Eliminated players continue to vote on submissions.
  • Unsubmitted votes are counted as accepts.
  • Duplicate words are rejected and the timer keeps running.
